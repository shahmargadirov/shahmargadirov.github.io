Sim-Cade Racing & Vehicle Simulation

MOTORSPORT is a racing game project focused on responsive driving physics, detailed vehicles, and immersive environments.

About the Project

The goal is to combine accessible sim-cade handling with detailed vehicle presentation and an engaging racing experience.

Inspired by Gran Turismo Series and Forza Horizon, MOTORSPORT aims to be the first Roblox game that showcases great detail for cars, for gameplay, and graphics.

I have worked on Motorsport for about 6 years now. During these years, I had learned Scripting, Animating, Modeling and desigining our UI Design.

Game is still in progress of development, and is expected to release in Early Spring of 2027. Our communications server has 1000+ members waiting patiently for our game to release.
