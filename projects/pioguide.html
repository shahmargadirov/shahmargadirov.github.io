PioGuide

iOS Campus Navigation & AI Academic Advising

PioGuide is a campus-focused application designed to help students navigate university buildings and better understand their academic progress. Explore the features and development highlights below.

Project Overview

PioGuide combines campus navigation with academic advising tools. Its planned features include detailed indoor routes, course requirement analysis, and recommendations to help students plan future semesters.
